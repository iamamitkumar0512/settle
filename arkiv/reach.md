# Reach

## Who is it for, and would they use it?

Settle is for small businesses and freelancers who bill international clients, such as a studio in
Mumbai billing a client in Berlin. They want USDT to reach their own wallet directly, and their
customers shouldn't need to understand crypto.

Arkiv's part is the invoice itself: the business name, description and customer details that make a
payment make sense to a person. Without it, a customer sees a wallet address and a number. An
accountant gets an export that says who paid for what.

We haven't tested Settle with real merchants yet, so we can't claim demand.
